Forge 1.2.0 is the first release published from the Forge-Music repository. It brings the complete Forge 1.1.0 feature set, with refreshed documentation and download links under the Forge name.

**Highlights**
- Home built around you: a daylist, mixes by genre, Discover mixes and listening streaks.
- Automix blends songs, matched in key and tempo.
- Bit-perfect output to USB DACs and the headphone jack, multichannel too.
- Forge for TV, a separate app, with your phone as its remote.
- Song comments, smart playlists, audiobooks, and picking several songs at once.
- Synced lyrics, offline downloads, live radio, podcasts and listening together.
- Add to several playlists at once; liked songs sync with YouTube Music.
- Redesigned pickers, settings and playlist pages.

**Downloads**
- `Forge-v1.2.0-arm64-v8a.apk` for most phones from the last several years.
- `Forge-v1.2.0-armeabi-v7a.apk` for older 32-bit phones.
- `Forge-v1.2.0-x86_64.apk` for emulators and x86 devices.
- `Forge-v1.2.0-universal.apk` when you are unsure which processor your phone uses.
- `Forge-TV-v1.2.0.apk` for Android TV and Google TV.
- `Forge-v1.2.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` (installer), `.msi`, or the portable `.zip` for Windows.
- `Forge-v1.2.0-macos-arm64.dmg` for Apple silicon Macs.
- `Forge-v1.2.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, `.deb`, or `.rpm` for Linux.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` with a checksum for every APK.

**Install**
Download the APK for your phone and open it. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager. Forge checks this repository for updates and can update itself from Settings.

Forge is free, has no ads and needs no account. Signing in is optional and only adds account-backed likes and playlists. The [README](https://github.com/lokesh12dev/Forge-Music#readme) has the full feature list, build instructions and the creator section.

The Android application ID is `com.forge`. This is the first release under this identity, so install it fresh; it cannot update a build with a different application ID.
